Vastu Compass — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Vastu Compass ("the app") is made by Jatin Dhir. This policy explains what the app does with information on your phone. In short: the app collects nothing, sends nothing anywhere, and shows no ads.

What the app uses, and why

  • Compass and motion sensors (magnetometer, gyroscope, accelerometer): to show the direction the phone points and whether it is level. Read live on the phone, never stored or sent.
  • Location (only if you allow it): to work out the difference between magnetic north and true north where you are, and for the optional GPS walk calibration. Your position is used on the phone only. The app keeps your last position on the phone so true north is ready next time you open it. It is never sent anywhere.
  • Your site readings: the names, directions and notes you save for a home or plot are stored only on your phone.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or advertising ID.
  • No network connections: the Google Play version of the app does not use the internet at all.
  • No sale or sharing of data with anyone.

Sharing

If you choose to export a site's readings, the app opens your phone's own share sheet and you pick where they go (for example a messaging app). Nothing is shared unless you do this yourself.

Deleting your data

Delete a site inside the app, or uninstall the app, and everything it stored is removed from your phone. You can also turn location permission off at any time in your phone's settings; the compass keeps working with magnetic north.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
